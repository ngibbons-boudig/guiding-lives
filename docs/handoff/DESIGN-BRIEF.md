## Brand identity

The logo is a soft watercolour mark: a young person's head in profile (sage green silhouette) with a hand-drawn heart on the temple, framed by a thin open circle and a pair of sage leaves either side, with a small golden sun and rays top-right.

**Lockup (top to bottom)**

1. Symbol (head, heart, circle, leaves, sun)
2. Wordmark: "Guiding Lives" in a dark green high-contrast serif, with a small leaf replacing the dot of the "i" in "Lives"
3. Subheading: "YOUTH MENTORING SUPPORT" in wide-tracked uppercase sans
4. A short hand-drawn brush rule
5. Strapline: "Connection first. Learning follows." in a handwritten script

**Usage notes**

- Primary lockup is stacked and centred, on the warm cream background (not pure white).
- A horizontal variant (symbol left, wordmark right) will be needed for the site header; this does not exist yet.
- The symbol works on its own as a favicon and social avatar.
- The strapline appears twice on the infographic (header and footer), so treat it as a standing brand element rather than a one-off headline.
- The infographic also carries a secondary line, "Supporting young people and families to build safer, brighter futures", suited to a hero sub-heading or meta description.
- Source files are flattened PNGs. Request vector (SVG/AI/PDF) versions and a transparent-background PNG before build.

## Typography

The brand uses three typefaces: a high-contrast serif for display, a wide-tracked geometric sans for labels, and a handwritten script for straplines. The exact fonts are not named in the files, so the table gives the closest free Google Fonts matches; confirm with Shelley before build.

| Role | Observed style | Suggested web font | Use on the site |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Display serif | High-contrast transitional serif, generous x-height ("Guiding Lives", "Our Model") | Playfair Display or Cormorant Garamond | H1 and H2, hero headline |
| Label sans | Light geometric sans, all caps, tracking around 0.2em ("YOUTH MENTORING SUPPORT", "FAMILY & CARERS") | Montserrat Light or Jost | Section eyebrows, nav, card titles, buttons |
| Body sans | Clean humanist sans at small sizes (bullet lists, paragraphs) | Same family as the label sans, Regular weight | Body copy, lists |
| Script | Flowing handwritten brush script ("Connection first. Learning follows.", "More than mentoring…") | Allura, Sacramento or Dancing Script | Straplines and pull quotes only; never for body or navigation |

**Rules**

- Headings in deep green #304337; eyebrows and labels in mid sage #898F76 or deep green.
- Script is decorative: limit it to one line per screen and keep it at 28px or larger for legibility.
- Generous line height (1.6 or more) and white space to match the calm feel of the infographic.

## Visual language

The look is soft, organic and hand-made: watercolour textures, botanical line art and natural landscape photography on a cream ground.

- **Backgrounds:** warm cream with faint paper or watercolour texture; sections separated by organic curved edges or torn-paper shapes rather than straight lines.
- **Illustration:** sage watercolour leaves and sprigs used as corner decorations and section dividers; a thin hand-drawn brush stroke under headings.
- **Photography:** natural, outdoor, coastal and meadow imagery (the infographic shows a clifftop coastline and a wildflower field). Soft light, no hard flash, no stock-office imagery. Existing site photos stay, but a grading pass to warm and desaturate them toward the palette is recommended.
- **Icons:** thin single-weight line icons in deep green, each sitting inside a pale sage circle (heart, leaf, house, people, shield-tick, book, lotus, handshake, document, graduation cap, cycle arrows, network nodes).
- **Shapes:** circles and blobs, not boxes. Cards in the infographic are irregular watercolour ovals; translate to rounded cards (24px+ radius) with soft shadows or no shadows.
- **Central motif:** the tree — young person at the trunk, six support areas as branches, foundations as roots. This is the brand's core diagram and should be rebuilt for the web (see content section).
