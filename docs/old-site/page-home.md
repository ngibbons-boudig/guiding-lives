## Welcome to

### Guiding Lives Youth Mentoring

Every young person deserves the chance to feel seen, supported, and valued. Through Guiding Lives Youth Mentoring, I offer one-to-one mentoring for young people aged 11 to 18 who may be experiencing anxiety, trauma, emotionally based school avoidance (EBSA), or difficulties with self-esteem and confidence.

My mentoring approach blends practical support with holistic tools to nurture emotional well-being and personal development. Every session is flexible and tailored to the young person’s needs — whether that’s outdoors, in the community, at home, or online.

## My Approach

My role is to create a safe, non-judgmental space where they can be heard, supported, and guided toward a healthier, more confident future.

[IMAGE: icon1.png alt=]

#### Core Vision

I believe every young person, regardless of background or circumstance, deserves access to the right guidance and opportunities to thrive. My focus is always on empowering them to build confidence, independence, and a strong sense of self.

[IMAGE: icon2.png alt=]

#### Holistic Practices

I combine traditional behaviour management with holistic methods such as mindfulness, breathwork, and creative expression. These approaches encourage calm, self-awareness, and genuine emotional balance.

[IMAGE: icon3.png alt=]

#### Blended Support

Each mentoring relationship is built on trust and consistency. By combining practical mentoring with holistic care, I meet each young person exactly where they are — emotionally, mentally, and educationally — helping them take positive steps toward change.
